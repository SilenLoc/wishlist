> The first history of the notebook, a simple invention that changed the way the world thinks. Roland Allen traces the notebook from Renaissance merchants' ledgers and Leonardo da Vinci's sketchbooks to mass-produced diaries and the Moleskine, showing how thinking on paper shaped art, science, commerce and everyday life. A New Yorker Best Book of the Year 2024.
