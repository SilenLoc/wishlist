> The dramatic true story of Theodore Roosevelt's harrowing 1914 exploration of the uncharted tributary of the Amazon River. After losing his bid for a third presidential term, Roosevelt embarked on a deadly journey through the Brazilian wilderness that tested his physical and mental limits. A gripping tale of survival, determination, and the high cost of adventure.
