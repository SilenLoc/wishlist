> A radical exploration of time management that confronts the reality that the average human lifespan is absurdly short—about four thousand weeks. Burkeman offers a philosophical approach to productivity, arguing that accepting our limitations can lead to a more meaningful life rather than chasing the impossible dream of "getting everything done".
