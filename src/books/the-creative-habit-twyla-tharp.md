# The Creative Habit - Twyla Tharp

**Category**: Books

> Legendary choreographer Twyla Tharp shares her practical insights on creativity and discipline. Through personal anecdotes and exercises, she reveals how creativity is not a gift but a habit that can be learned and practised. The book provides a systematic approach to developing a creative routine, overcoming blocks, and sustaining artistic growth throughout one's life.
